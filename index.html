FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from Queens homeowners and business owners facing water damage restoration needs.

How quickly can you respond to a water damage emergency?

We offer 24/7 water damage repair and aim to dispatch a technician to your Queens property as quickly as possible after your call, since fast action limits damage and reduces the risk of mold.

Do you handle both residential and commercial water damage?

Yes. We handle both residential water damage in single-family homes, apartments, and multi-unit buildings, as well as offices, retail spaces, and other commercial properties throughout Queens.

What causes basement flooding, and can you clean it up?

Basement flooding is commonly caused by heavy rain, sump pump failure, or burst pipes. Our basement flood cleanup team extracts standing water, dries the space thoroughly, and helps prevent mold and structural issues.

Can you repair the source of the water damage, like a burst pipe?

We provide burst pipe repair support, including extraction, drying, and restoration of affected areas, so your property is fully dried out and restored.
